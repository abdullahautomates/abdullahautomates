# ParkLens

An AI parking assistant built by Abdullah Mustafa, Eshal Nadeem, and Anushuya at the University of Southern Mississippi's Hatchathon in November 2026.

## The problem

A student needs to get to class. Their parking permit allows some lots, their class is near others, and an allowed lot may already be full.

## The workflow

1. Read the student's next class.
2. Check which lots their permit allows.
3. Use computer vision to count open and occupied spaces in parking lot footage.
4. Pick one lot and open directions in Google Maps.

Students can also ask where to park by voice. Voice interaction uses ElevenLabs.

## Scope

The prototype runs on recorded campus footage. Live camera integration is a next step. It was built by a team of three; it is not a deployed campus parking service.
